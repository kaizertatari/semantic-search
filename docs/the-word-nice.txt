"Nice" started out as an insult. It comes from Latin nescius, meaning ignorant, and entered English meaning foolish or silly. Over a few centuries it drifted through shy, then fussy, then precise (a nice distinction), and only fairly recently landed on pleasant.

What I like about it is the direction of travel. The word got blander every time it moved. There's probably a general rule in there about words that get used constantly: heavy use sands the edges off.
